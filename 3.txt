DOCUMENT 3 — PARTICIPANT IDENTIFIER

The participant identifier for this application is the email address that you will enter in the Google Form.

CANONICAL EMAIL RULE — REVISION 04

Before hashing, transform the email string as follows:

1. Remove whitespace from the beginning and end.
2. Convert all remaining characters to lowercase.
3. In the local part (the text before `@`), remove every period (`.`).
4. In the local part, remove the plus-tag: the `+` character and everything after it up to `@`.
5. Do not modify the domain or substitute another address.

In compact form:

canonical_email = remove_plus_tag_and_periods(lowercase(trim(email)))

Use the original email address in the Google Form. The canonical form is used only to calculate the digest; the organizers will apply the same normalization before checking it.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A cryptographic hash operates on bytes. Two strings that look similar to a person can produce completely different digests if even one character differs. Normalization makes the procedure repeatable. For example, `Example.Name+tag@Mail.com` canonicalizes to `examplename@mail.com`.

The next step is in `4.txt`. It describes the SHA-256 operation and gives you a browser-based option.
